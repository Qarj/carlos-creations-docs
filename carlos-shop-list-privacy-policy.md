# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Carlos Shop List is designed to store your shopping list on your device.

## What The App Stores

The app stores the shopping list items you create, including item names and priority status.

If you use the import or export feature, the app also creates or reads an export file inside the app's internal storage on your device.

## What The App Does Not Do

Carlos Shop List does not require an account.

Carlos Shop List does not send your shopping list data to the developer.

Carlos Shop List does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used only to:

- display and manage your shopping list in the app
- save your list locally on your device
- support the app's import and export feature

## Data Sharing

Carlos Shop List does not sell, share, or transmit your shopping list data to third parties.

## Data Deletion

You can delete items from the app at any time.

If you uninstall the app, the app's local data is removed as part of Android's normal app removal process.
