# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

Carlos Pointy Arrow is designed to store your favourite waypoints on your device.

## What The App Stores

The app stores the waypoints you create, including names and coordinates.

If you use the import or export feature, the app also creates or reads an export file inside the app's internal storage on your device.

## What The App Does Not Do

Carlos Pointy Arrow does not require an account.

Carlos Pointy Arrow does not send your waypoint data to the developer.

Carlos Pointy Arrow does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used only to:

- display and manage your waypoints in the app
- save your waypoints locally on your device
- support the app's import and export feature

## Data Sharing

Carlos Pointy Arrow does not sell, share, or transmit your waypoint data to third parties.

## Data Deletion

You can delete waypoints from the app at any time.

If you uninstall the app, the app's local data is removed as part of Android's normal app removal process.
