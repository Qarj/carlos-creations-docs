# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Carlos Tube Stations is designed to help you find the nearest London Underground stations while keeping your data on your device.

## What The App Uses

The app uses your device location and compass-related sensors so it can calculate the nearest stations, live direction, magnetic heading, and GPS accuracy shown on screen.

The app includes a built-in list of Tube stations packaged inside the app.

## What The App Does Not Do

Carlos Tube Stations does not require an account.

Carlos Tube Stations does not send your live location or location history to the developer.

Carlos Tube Stations does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Carlos Tube Stations does not require an internet connection to work.

Carlos Tube Stations does not ask you to create or upload personal content.

## How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used only to:

- calculate the nearest Tube stations on-device
- show direction, heading, and GPS accuracy in the app
- display station information from the built-in station list

## Data Sharing

Carlos Tube Stations does not sell, share, or transmit your location data to third parties.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Carlos Tube Stations is designed for live on-device use and does not keep a location history for the developer.

If you uninstall the app, Android removes the app's local data as part of the normal app removal process.
